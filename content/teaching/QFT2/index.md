## 🧮 Topics Covered

The course builds on QFT I and includes:

- Review of relativistic quantum fields  
- Spinor fields and Dirac equation in QFT  
- Gauge symmetry and gauge fields  
- Quantum electrodynamics (QED)  
- Renormalization and regularization  
- Loop corrections and Feynman diagrams  
- Ward identities and symmetry constraints  
- Introduction to non-Abelian gauge theories  

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- Understand the structure of interacting quantum field theories  
- Perform perturbative calculations in gauge theories  
- Apply renormalization techniques  
- Analyze scattering amplitudes beyond tree level  
- Prepare for research in particle physics, cosmology, and quantum gravity
